Professional Summary

Associate Professor of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Kangwon National University, directing Bae’s Integrated Circuits and Systems Lab (BICS). Technical Program Committee member of IEEE ISSCC (since 2018) and IEEE A-SSCC (since 2021). Research on energy-efficient mixed-signal integrated circuits and systems for biomedical sensors, neural interfaces, and body-area networks.
