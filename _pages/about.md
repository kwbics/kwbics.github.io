Welcome to **KANGWON BICS** — _Bae's Integrated Circuits and Systems Lab_ at Kangwon National University. We study **mixed-signal integrated circuits and system architectures** for neural interfaces, FPGA-based sensor systems, and high-speed wireline links. Our work spans low-noise recording and stimulation front-ends, wireless power and data links for implants, sensor-to-FPGA streaming data paths, and transmitter, receiver, and clocking circuits for serial interfaces.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
**Joonsung Bae** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Kangwon National University, which he joined as an Assistant Professor in 2017. He received the B.S. (2007), M.S. (2009), and Ph.D. (2013) degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST. He was a Senior Engineer at Samsung Electronics (Memory Business, 2013–2014), working on PCI Express 3.0 and M-PHY 3.0 PHYs, a postdoctoral researcher at KAIST (2014–2015), and a research staff member at [imec](https://www.imec-int.com/){:target="_blank"}, Belgium (2015–2017), where he served as a project leader in neural sensing and stimulation IC development.

His research spans analog, RF, and mixed-signal circuits for biomedical and neural interfaces, ultra-low-power wireless links for medical devices, AI-enabled sensor systems, and medical imaging. He has served on the Technical Program Committees of IEEE ISSCC and IEEE A-SSCC. His honors include the Outstanding Researcher of the Month Award from the Industry-University Cooperation Foundation of Kangwon National University (December 2022) and the Best Design Award (1st prize) of the IEEE A-SSCC Student Design Contest in 2011 and, as a co-recipient, in 2014.

Since 2024, he has also been a co-founder and CIO of XO Semiconductor (xosemi), where he works on future technology development, including medical imaging sensor circuits, high-speed interface design, and FPGA-based data capture. This industry work connects with the lab's research on neural interfaces, FPGA-based sensor systems, and high-speed wireline links.
