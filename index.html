Welcome to KANGWON BICS — Bae’s Integrated Circuits and Systems Lab at Kangwon National University. We design energy-efficient mixed-signal integrated circuits and systems for IoT, bio-medical sensors, neural interfaces, and body-area networks. Our chips sense, stimulate, and communicate — spanning body channel communication, wireless power transfer, neural recording/stimulation front-ends, bio-impedance and ultrasound sensing, and high-speed interfaces.

Joonsung Bae is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Kangwon National University. He received the B.S. (2007), M.S. (2009), and Ph.D. (2013) degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST. He was a Senior Engineer at Samsung Electronics (Memory Business, 2013–2014), an R&D researcher at imec, Belgium (2015–2017), and joined Kangwon National University in 2017. He serves on the Technical Program Committees of IEEE ISSCC (since 2018) and IEEE A-SSCC (since 2021), and received the A-SSCC Best Design Award in 2011 and 2014.
